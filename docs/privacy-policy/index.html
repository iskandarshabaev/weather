Privacy Policy

App: Weather  ·  Developer: Iskandar Shabaev
Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Weather Android application processes information to provide weather forecasts.

Information used by the app

Weather allows you to look up forecasts for cities you select. The app does not require an account and does not request access to your device's GPS location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

To retrieve forecasts, the app sends weather-related requests, including the city being queried, to an external weather information service. Like other internet requests, these requests may reveal technical information such as your IP address to that service.

How information is used

Information is used to retrieve and display weather forecasts and support app functionality. The app does not use city selections for advertising.

Local storage and deletion

Weather may keep selected cities and app-related settings on your device so that you can use the app conveniently. You can delete the app's local data through Android system settings or by uninstalling the app. This does not delete information independently retained by the external weather provider.

Third-party services and sharing

Weather communicates with an external weather data provider to obtain forecasts. The requested city and technical network information, which may include your IP address, may be processed by that provider under its own privacy practices. The developer does not sell your personal information. Weather does not intentionally share user information with advertising partners.

Children

Weather is a general-purpose weather application and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app or its data practices change. The updated version and its effective date will appear on this page.
